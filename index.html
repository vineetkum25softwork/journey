My Journey

Quick Summary

After completing my BBA in 2019, I planned and tried launching a tech startup, but it failed during the pandemic testing phase. I then self-taught full-stack coding, overcame a period of confusion and hesitation, and planned, built, and launched a portfolio of paid, affiliate-driven digital products in 2026.

Short Summary

2019: Sparked the idea for digital voucher sharing after completing my BBA, building a basic tool near October to check interest and gather feedback.
2020: From late 2019 to early 2020, pitched to store owners and finalized the web app in September.
2021: Tested and pitched the app, but adoption failed due to pandemic-related low footfall and delays, leading me to pause the startup.
Mid 2021 to 2023: Dedicated 2.5 years to mastering front-end and back-end web development and launching live projects.
2024 to 2025: Remained confused and stuck for 1.5 years, planning businesses but unable to take action due to self-doubt and fear of failure, before gaining clarity to build 6 projects.
2026: Launched initial projects sequentially under a free-to-use affiliate revenue model, but hit distribution limits due to low social traffic, vague audience reach on general directories, and a lack of incentive for content creators to promote them. Pivoted to a paid model with direct publisher affiliate incentives to acquire targeted customers efficiently, focusing efforts on paid-with-affiliate products like Affistrike, MockOverflow, and ScrollStrike.

Detailed Timeline

2019: The Spark

• Sparked the concept of digital voucher sharing after seeing paper coupons and SMS vouchers.
• The idea was to create a city-based voucher wallet app where each customer would get a dedicated space to instantly store vouchers from local stores, browse active offers across the city, and redeem them later, while merchants could send branded vouchers after billing through a one-time campaign setup.
• Built a basic version for merchants to share digital vouchers with customers to encourage repeat visits and redemptions near October.
• Used this basic app to explain the concept to owners and ask if they were interested in such a concept and what more features could be added.
• Gathered these improvement suggestions to develop a final product valuable enough to sell via subscription.

2020: Development & Testing

- From the last months of 2019 to the first few months of 2020, pitched the concept to many store owners; a few liked the idea & gave suggestions.
- Overcame technical delays to finish the full web application by September 2020.

2021: The Pandemic Pivot

• From late 2020 to early 2021, tested by pitching the app; while some were interested, low footfall due to COVID-19 prevented adoption.
• Some business owners delayed operations, planning to restart only when the time was right.
• Paused the startup and pivoted to learning professional programming to build my own future.

Mid 2021 – 2023: Learning the Craft

• Spent 2.5 years mastering programming, web development, and modern frameworks.
• Gained deep technical expertise in both Front-end and Back-end development.
• Built several local projects and launched two live apps to gain real-world experience.
• At that time, I believed the self-taught path would be better for learning the practical skills needed to build digital products (script, plugin, templates, browser extention, webapp & many kind of digital product) and start a business.
• Learned through YouTube, hands-on practice, project development, and researching solutions through Stack Overflow and other developer resources.

2024 – 2025: Research & Execution

• Researched niche markets, digital products, and AI-driven tools throughout 2024.
• Made plans for websites to start but remained stuck and confused for 1.5 years, unable to take action due to fear of challenges, self-doubt, and fear of failure.
• Gained full clarity by mid-2025 and planned 6 distinct website projects.
• Finally broke through the fear and developed all 6 projects and various digital products between August and January.

2026: Launch Phase & Strategic Pivot

• Launched Affirzone, Fegoprint, Affistrike, and MockOverflow sequentially, but hit a distribution wall: social media traffic was too low, general website directories brought vague and un-targeted audiences, and niche blogs/websites had zero financial incentive to feature or promote free tools.
• Realized a paid model with direct publisher affiliate incentives works far better, so converted Affistrike and MockOverflow to paid-with-affiliates, added affiliate rewards to the paid ScrollStrike plugin, and paused Fegoprint and Affirzone to focus on this strategy.
