My Journey (Detailed)

2019: The Spark

• Sparked the concept of digital voucher sharing after seeing paper coupons and SMS offers.
• Built a basic version for merchants to share digital vouchers with customers to encourage repeat visits and redemptions near October.
• Used this basic app to explain the concept to owners and ask if they were interested in such a concept and what more features could be added.
• Gathered these improvement suggestions to develop a final product valuable enough to sell via subscription.

2020: Development & Testing

• From the last months of 2019 to the starting few months of 2020, pitched the concept to store owners who liked the idea and gave suggestions using the app.
• Overcame technical delays to finish the full web application by September 2020.

2021: The Pandemic Pivot

• From late 2020 to early 2021, tested by pitching the app; while some were interested, low footfall due to COVID-19 prevented adoption.
• Some business owners delayed operations, planning to restart only when the time was right.
• Paused the startup and pivoted to learning professional programming to build my own future.

Mid 2021 – 2023: Learning the Craft

• Spent 2.5 years mastering programming, web development, and modern frameworks.
• Gained deep technical expertise in both Front-end and Back-end development.
• Built several local projects and launched two live apps to gain real-world experience.

2024 – 2025: Research & Execution

• Researched niche markets, digital products, and AI-driven tools throughout 2024.
• Made plans for websites to start but remained stuck and confused for 1.5 years, unable to take action due to fear of challenges and failure.
• Gained full clarity by mid-2025 and planned 6 distinct website projects.
• Finally broke through the fear and developed all 6 projects and various digital products between August and January.

2026: Launch Phase

• February to May: Launching each product one by one with a detailed marketing plan.
• Focusing on content creation and utilizing Pinterest as a key traffic strategy.
• Finalizing monetization plans to turn the built projects into a sustainable business.

Short Summary

2019: Sparked the idea for digital voucher sharing and built a basic tool near October to check interest in the concept and gather feature suggestions.
2020: From the final months of 2019 to early 2020, I pitched to owners and later finalized the web app in September.
2021: Pitched the app from late 2020 to early 2021; adoption failed due to pandemic-related low footfall and store owners waiting for better timing.
Mid 2021 to 2023: Dedicated 2.5 years to mastering front-end and back-end web development and launching live projects.
2024 to 2025: Remained stuck for 1.5 years due to fear of failure before gaining mid-2025 clarity to develop 6 website projects and various digital products from August to January.
2026: Launching each product one by one from February to May, focusing on Pinterest, content creation, and structured marketing strategies.
